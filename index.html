About me

    

      

        
I'm Abdullahi Hassan Abdinoor. I studied at M-PESA Foundation Academy and I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at Africa International University (AIU) in Kenya.

        
I'm building my foundation in technology while exploring software development, networking, databases, systems administration, and digital problem-solving. I learn best through practical projects rather than theory alone, from programming and database design to networking simulations, web development, and productivity tools.

        
Beyond technology, I'm interested in entrepreneurship, business development, sales, and digital marketing. I want to build businesses that solve real problems in Kenya and across Africa, and to grow from learning technical skills into building useful products with other talented people.

I believe in continuous learning, practical experience, discipline, and building things that provide genuine value.
